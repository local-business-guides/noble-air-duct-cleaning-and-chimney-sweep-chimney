What chimney sweeping is for

A chimney sweep service cleans deposits from parts of a chimney that carry smoke away from a home. Soot and creosote can collect inside a flue as a fireplace is used. The amount and type of buildup depend on the chimney and its condition, so a provider should explain what cleaning is appropriate for the particular setup.

The details depend on the chimney and the provider's scope. Before scheduling, ask what parts will be cleaned and what is not included. Common terms you'll hear include:
