Terms you may hear during chimney care

A few common words can make a service discussion easier:
- Flue: The channel that carries smoke and gases out through a chimney.
- Soot: Dark residue produced by burning.
- Damper: A movable part that opens or closes the passage to the flue.

If a provider uses a term you do not recognize, ask what it means for the work at your home. Clear explanations are a practical part of comparing services.
