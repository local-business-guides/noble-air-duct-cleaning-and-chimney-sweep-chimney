Scope, timing, and key terms

The exact process varies with the home's duct layout and the service agreed on. Before booking, get a clear outline of which areas will be addressed. Here are a few terms that may come up:

  • Supply duct: a duct that carries conditioned air from the system toward rooms.
  • Return duct: a duct that carries air back toward the heating and cooling equipment.
  • Register: the grille or opening where air enters or leaves a room.

Keep these key facts in mind as you plan:

  • What it covers: cleaning of the duct areas included in the provider's stated scope. Confirm whether registers, returns, or other components are included.
  • Typical timing: the time needed varies based on system size, layout, access, and the condition of the ductwork. Ask how long to set aside for your appointment.
  • What affects cost: the system's size and accessibility, the amount of material to remove, and any requested work beyond the basic scope can affect the estimate. Ask for a cost explanation for your home, since there is no single figure that applies to every system.
